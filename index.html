            Friday's original plan: Venice, the canals & Abbot Kinney

            These were the first-draft ideas for Friday. They're kept here, comments and all, for anyone who wants them another time.

            IdeaVenice Beach Boardwalk & Muscle Beach1.0 mi from the hotel

            Abbot Kinney founded Venice in 1905 as a seaside resort he called “Venice of America.” Its beachfront promenade, Ocean Front Walk, runs about 2.5 miles, and on a Friday afternoon it's a parade of vendors, murals, performers and people-watching like nowhere else.

            Muscle Beach Venice, the open-air weightlifting platform on the sand, carries on the Muscle Beach tradition that sparked America's fitness boom back in 1934. Grab a spot on the sand for the 4:50 PM sunset, then head off the boardwalk while it's still light.

            Don't miss
            • The lifters at the Muscle Beach platform
            • The murals and the palm-lined promenade
            • Sunset over the Pacific at 4:50 PM
            Time hereAbout an hour and a half, sunset included
            Good to knowBring sunglasses and a light layer; it cools off fast once the sun drops. Leave the boardwalk right after sunset.
            →Hotel → Muscle Beach: 1.0 mi · about 3 min by car
            Comments
              IdeaVenice CanalsA quiet storybook walk

              A ten-minute walk inland, the noise of the boardwalk gives way to something completely different: still water, little footbridges, gardens and canal-front homes. These are the surviving canals Abbot Kinney dug in 1905; many others were filled in for roads in 1929, and the rest were restored in 1992–93.

              In the soft light just after sunset it's one of the prettiest walks in LA, and it's quiet and residential.

              Don't miss
              • Crossing the footbridges for photos of the water
              • The variety of houses, from tiny cottages to modern glass
              • The afterglow until about 5:15 PM
              Time here30 to 45 minutes
              Good to knowThis is a residential neighborhood, so keep voices down. If it's already dark when you finish at the beach, take a short Uber instead of walking.
              →Muscle Beach → Canals: walk 0.5 mi · about 10 min, in twilight

                IdeaAbbot Kinney BlvdShops, coffee and some of the Westside’s best restaurants

                Named for Venice's founder, Abbot Kinney Boulevard is a mile of independent shops, galleries, cafés and some of the most talked-about restaurants on the Westside. It's walkable, well lit and busy on a Friday evening.

                Pop into a shop or two as you arrive (some close around 6 PM), then settle in for dinner. You'll want a reservation for four tonight.

                    Saturday · Nov 14

                    Hollywood Hills to Santa Monica

                    The big day, and now we drive it ourselves. We head up early to Griffith Observatory, the white Art Deco landmark on the hillside, and walk the fire road to the top of Mount Hollywood for the classic look across at the Hollywood Sign. Then down to Hollywood Boulevard for the stars and the Chinese Theatre, a wander-and-graze lunch at the 1934 Farmers Market, and, if time allows, a quick look at the Santa Monica Pier. We're back at the hotel by about 5 to shower and change, then Uber to Citrin for a Michelin-star finale so nobody has to drive.

                    10:00 AMLeave the MarriottDrive to Griffith · ~40–50 min on a Saturday
                    ➤To Griffith

                    Grab breakfast and coffee, pull the SUV around from the valet and head northeast across town. The Observatory opens at 10, and on a Saturday the drive from the marina usually runs 40 to 50 minutes, sometimes more as the hill road fills up, which is exactly why we go early.

                    Bring
                    • Water for everyone
                    • Sunscreen and hats; the trail has little shade
                    • Grippy sneakers
                    • A card for the parking machine (no cash)
                    Comments
                      10:45 – 1:00Griffith + Mount Hollywood hike~3 mi round trip · ~500 ft · moderate
                      ➤Directions➤Greek Theatre✆Call

                      Here's how it works. We park at the Observatory and walk from the north end of the parking area onto the Charlie Turner Trail, a wide dirt fire road that crosses over the Vermont Canyon road tunnel and climbs steadily around the mountain to the summit of Mount Hollywood. Then we come back the same way. It's about 3 miles round trip with about 500 ft of climb, roughly an hour and a half, and moderate rather than hard.

                      We don't hike to the Hollywood Sign itself. From the trail and the summit you look straight across at the sign on Mount Lee, the next peak over, along with a 360-degree view of the LA basin, downtown and, on clear days, the ocean.

                      Don't miss
                      • Hollywood Sign views across the canyon
                      • Dante's View, a garden spot near the top with a water fountain and picnic tables
                      • The summit panorama: downtown, the basin and the San Gabriel Mountains
                      Easy on the joints: stay on the wide main trail both ways and skip the steep single-track shortcut that starts with rocky steps just past the saddle.
                      Parking
                      • Top lot & Western CanyonPaid 10 AM–10 PM

                        Pay-and-display machines, cards only. The rate is on the machine.

                      • Backup: Greek TheatreFree when there's no show (none found today)

                        Park below and ride the DASH shuttle up for 50¢.

                      • Lower Fern DellOften free

                        Check the posted signs.

                      Time hereAbout 1½ hours on the trail, plus the Observatory
                      Good to knowRestrooms are next to the Observatory parking lot; use them before you start. Little shade, so water, sunscreen and hats. Phones often lose signal up here, so agree on a meeting spot.
                      Comments
                        In the Griffith blockInside Griffith ObservatoryFree · open 10 AM–10 PM today

                        Before or after the hike, step inside. Admission has been free since the Observatory opened in 1935, as Griffith J. Griffith's will required, and it's one of the great free experiences in LA. The Art Deco building has science and space exhibits, the Samuel Oschin Planetarium (once used to train Apollo astronauts in celestial navigation) and terraces looking over downtown, Hollywood and the Pacific.

                        If you've seen “Rebel Without a Cause,” you'll recognize the place.

                        Don't miss
                        • Peek through the free Zeiss and solar telescopes in the copper domes (when the sky is clear)
                        • The view from the terraces and roof walkways
                        • Check planetarium show times as you arrive
                        Time hereAbout 45 minutes
                        Good to knowOpen Saturday 10 AM–10 PM. No food or drinks inside the building; the Café at the End of the Universe is on site.
                        Comments
                          1:15 – 2:15Hollywood Blvd & TCL ChineseWalk of Fame · keep it short
                          ➤To the garage

                          Hollywood Boulevard is busy, touristy and a little wild, and you have to see it once. More than 2,800 terrazzo-and-brass stars line the boulevard, and the heart of it is the pagoda-topped TCL Chinese Theatre (still widely called Grauman's Chinese), whose forecourt holds the handprints, footprints and signatures of movie stars set in concrete since the 1920s. Right next door is the Dolby Theatre, home of the Academy Awards. Keep this one short: an hour is plenty.

                          Don't miss
                          • Finding your favorite stars' handprints in the Chinese Theatre forecourt
                          • Spotting names on the Walk of Fame
                          • The Dolby Theatre, where the Oscars happen
                          ParkingOvation Hollywood garage, 6801 Hollywood Blvd. Enter from Highland (Johnny Grant Way) or Orange Dr. $3 for up to 2 hours with a shop or restaurant validation; otherwise $7.50/hr, $25 max.
                          Good to knowIt's crowded, so keep phones and wallets close.
                          Comments
                            2:30 – 3:45Lunch at the Original Farmers Market3rd & Fairfax · get your parking validated
                            ➤Directions✆Call

                            It started in 1934, when a dozen farmers parked their trucks on an old dairy ranch at 3rd and Fairfax to sell produce. Today it's a beloved maze of food stalls, sit-down spots, bakeries, grocers and shops under the famous white clock tower, and an official Los Angeles Historic-Cultural Monument. It's the perfect group lunch: everyone wanders off for whatever looks good and meets back at a shared table. The Grove is right next door if anyone wants a stroll.

                            ParkingUse the Farmers Market lot, not The Grove garage (validations don't cross). Ask for validation when you buy food.
                            HoursOpen today 10 AM–9 PM
                            Good to knowSaturday lunch is busy, so grab a table as soon as you see one.
                            Comments
                              4:00 – 4:50Santa Monica Pier & PromenadeOnly if we're on timeOptional
                              ➤Directions

                              If we're on time, a quick taste of where LA meets the ocean. Palisades Park runs along the bluff above the beach on Ocean Avenue, and below it the pier, which dates to 1909, starts to glow as the sun drops: Pacific Park's Ferris wheel lights up and the old carousel spins inside the Hippodrome. Sunset is about 4:50 PM.

                              Keep it to a short stroll, then head back to the hotel. Running late? Skip this and go straight to the Marriott; the shower stop comes first.

                              Don't miss

                              • The 1922 carousel in the Hippodrome

                                                If you'd rather: alternatives

                                                Getty Center11.9 mi · ~19 min from the hotel

                                                Art, gardens and city views. Free admission with a timed-entry reservation. Saturday 10 AM–9 PM. Parking is $25 per car. Some galleries are closed for renovation.

                                                Runyon Canyon12.4 mi · ~29 min

                                                Shorter Hollywood hike from 2000 N Fuller Ave, two blocks from Hollywood Boulevard. The 1.9-mile loop climbs about 500 ft, but its ridge is steep with wooden steps; the wide fire road is the gentler way.

                                                Baldwin Hills Scenic OverlookCulver City · ~14 min from the hotel

                                                Easy loop around a 420-ft summit with a panorama of the LA basin, the ocean and the mountains. The 282-step staircase is very steep; the Hillside Trail beside it is a gentler 0.6 mi. Open 8 AM to sunset. No good Hollywood Sign view.

                                                Warner Bros. Studio Tour Hollywood23.2 mi · ~34 min

                                                About 3 hours, including the Friends Central Perk set. Tours 8:30 AM–3:30 PM; from $79 for age 11+.
